Here is a summary of the standardized pricing and competition limits:

1. Pricing Plans (Pricing Cards & Database)
   - Free Plan (£0): 
     - 1 live competition (`maxActiveRaffles: 1`).
     - Access to Instant Wins excluded.
   - Premium Plan (£29/month):
     - Up to 3 live competitions (`maxActiveRaffles: 3`).
     - Access to Instant Wins included.
     - 3 monthly featured listing slots.
   - Pro Plan (£79/month):
     - Unlimited live competitions (`maxActiveRaffles: null`).
     - Access to Instant Wins included.
     - Unlimited featured listing slots, custom branding, and advanced analytics.

2. Pricing Comparison (Matrix Table)
   - Updated "Live competitions" row values to reflect standardized plan limits (Free: 1, Premium: Up to 3, Pro: Unlimited).
   - Instant Wins availability clearly mapped to Premium and Pro tiers.

3. Backend & Database Enforcement
   - Live competition limits enforced in `RafflesService.create()` against active/pending competitions.
   - Database plans updated in PostgreSQL via Prisma seeding.
