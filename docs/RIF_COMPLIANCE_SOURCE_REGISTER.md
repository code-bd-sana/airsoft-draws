## 1. Statutory Legislation & Statutory Instruments

### 1.1. Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (VCRA 2006) — Part 2, Sections 36–40
* **Organisation:** UK Parliament / Her Majesty's Stationery Office (HMSO)
* **Direct URL:** [https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/38/part/2/crossheading/imitation-firearms](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/38/part/2/crossheading/imitation-firearms)
* **Date Accessed:** 2026-08-20
* **Requirements Supported:**
  * **Section 36:** Prohibits the manufacture, importation, and sale/supply of Realistic Imitation Firearms (RIFs). Supply includes prize distribution and transfers.
  * **Section 37:** Establishes statutory defences for permitted activities (including airsoft skirmishing at insured sites).
  * **Section 38:** Defines a Realistic Imitation Firearm (RIF) as an imitation firearm with an appearance indistinguishable for practical purposes from a real firearm.
  * **Section 40:** Age restrictions prohibiting the sale, supply, or transfer of imitation firearms and RIFs to individuals under 18 years of age.

---

### 1.2. Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Realistic Imitation Firearms) Regulations 2007 (SI 2007/2606)
* **Organisation:** UK Home Office / UK Government
* **Direct URL:** [https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2606](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2606)
* **Date Accessed:** 2026-08-20
* **Requirements Supported:**
  * Defines specific conditions for the airsoft skirmishing defence: requires holding third-party public liability insurance and regular attendance at an airsoft skirmish site (typically 3 or more skirmishes in not less than 56 days).
  * Establishes two-tone color specifications (at least 51% bright red, orange, yellow, green, pink, purple, or blue) for non-realistic imitation firearms (IFs).

---

### 1.3. Home Office Guide on Firearms Licensing Law & VCRA Circulars
* **Organisation:** UK Home Office
* **Direct URL:** [https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/firearms-law-guidance-to-the-police-2012/guide-on-firearms-licensing-law-accessible-version](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/firearms-law-guidance-to-the-police-2012/guide-on-firearms-licensing-law-accessible-version)
* **Date Accessed:** 2026-08-20
* **Requirements Supported:**
  * Clarifies kinetic energy thresholds: airsoft devices capable of automatic fire operating below 1.3 Joules (or 2.5 Joules for single-shot) are not section 1 firearms, but remain governed under VCRA 2006 RIF controls.
  * Details legal obligations on suppliers and merchants regarding age verification (18+) prior to transfer.

## 2. Industry Body & Verification Sources

### 2.1. UKARA (United Kingdom Airsoft Retailers Association) Official Guidance
* **Organisation:** UKARA
* **Direct URL:** [https://www.ukara.org.uk/](https://www.ukara.org.uk/)
* **Date Accessed:** 2026-08-20
* **Requirements Supported:**
  * Clarifies that **UKARA IS NOT A LICENCE** or statutory permit. It is a centralised database of verified airsoft skirmishers maintained by retailers as evidence supporting a VCRA Section 37 legal defence.
  * Defines the requirement to verify that a winner's UKARA registration is active, current, and precisely matches the winner's full legal name, date of birth, and home address before releasing any RIF prize.

## 3. Advertising & Marketing Regulations

### 3.1. ASA (Advertising Standards Authority) & CAP Code Guidance on Weapons & Minors
* **Organisation:** Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) / Committee of Advertising Practice (CAP)
* **Direct URL:** [https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/weapons-general.html](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/weapons-general.html)
* **Date Accessed:** 2026-08-20
* **Requirements Supported:**
  * CAP Code Section 3 & Section 15: Marketing communications must not depict weapons irresponsibly, glamourise violence, use threatening imagery, or appeal particularly to children or minors under 18.
  * Requires clear disclosure of 18+ entry restrictions, terms and conditions, and legal defence checks in promotional material.
  * Prohibits misleading descriptions (e.g. describing UKARA as a "licence").

## 4. Privacy, Security & Data Protection Guidance

### 4.1. ICO UK GDPR Guidance on Principles, Data Minimisation & Security
* **Organisation:** Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
* **Direct URL:** [https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/)
* **Direct URL (Data Security):** [https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/security/a-guide-to-data-security/](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/security/a-guide-to-data-security/)
* **Date Accessed:** 2026-08-20
* **Requirements Supported:**
  * Government-issued ID documents (passports, driving licences) contain sensitive personal data.
  * Requires strict data minimisation: do not collect or log full DOB/ID details unnecessarily.
  * Mandates private, encrypted storage for identity documents, role-based access control, short-lived signed URLs, masking sensitive attributes by default, and establishing defined retention/purge schedules.
